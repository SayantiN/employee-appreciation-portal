Breakpoints that this product must survive
320–374iPhone SE, small Android. The hardest case — and the one the no-horizontal-scroll rule is measured against.
375–429iPhone 13–15, Pixel.
430–767Pro Max, large Android, small tablets portrait.
768–1023iPad. Drawer navigation. Tables still become cards below this line.
1024–1279iPad Pro landscape, small laptops. Icon rail.
1280–1535Standard desktop. Expanded rail.
≥ 1536Wide monitors. Content max-width capped and centred rather than stretched.
Release gate. Every screen is checked at each width in portrait and landscape on current Chrome, Safari, Edge and Firefox §12.3.
