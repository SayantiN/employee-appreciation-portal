Why the threshold exists
On a four-person team, a single anonymous vote is not anonymous. The nominee can work out who wrote it from the fact that it exists.
Rule. Feedback becomes visible only when the cycle has closed and the nominee received at least 3 votes in it.
min_feedback_reveal_count = 3 · R-6.7.2
What is never shown to the nominee
✗ Name
✗ Email
✗ Avatar or photo
✗ Department — identifying on a small team
✗ Timestamp finer than the date
And it is not only the UI. The API response behind this page carries no voter identifier in any form NF-10, AC-4.
Reporting a card hides it immediately for the reader and queues it for admin review R-6.7.4.
