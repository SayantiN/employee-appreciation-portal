Automatic. A scheduled job opens and closes cycles at their timestamps. Manual open or close is an override, and is logged R-6.16.2.
Reopening needs a typed reason, notifies everyone, and invalidates the frozen tally. It is an exception, not routine R-6.16.4.
Guards. One cycle per type and period. A cycle cannot open with fewer than 3 active employees — anonymity would be impossible R-6.16.1, R-6.16.5.
